# Catalogue artwork

`cover.png` is original geometric artwork generated by `scripts/create_cardtofile_cover.py`.
It contains no user data, screenshots of private accounts, logos or third-party imagery.

(`app/COPYING`). It may be stored and displayed in the OpenCloud app catalogue
under that license. The full license is included in the submission bundle.
